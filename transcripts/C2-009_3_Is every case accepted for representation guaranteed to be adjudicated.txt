After acceptance, the process reaches “Await client instruction.” It can then follow either:

- “Withdrawal instruction received” → “Archive non-adjudicated matter,” or
- “5 business days” → “Close matter for client non-response” → “Archive non-adjudicated matter.”

Therefore, acceptance for representation does not guarantee adjudication.
